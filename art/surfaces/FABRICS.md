The clothing materials use CC0 scans from Poly Haven. Each uses the photographed diffuse map converted to grayscale for tinting and the OpenGL normal map resized for the game.

- [Cotton Jersey](https://polyhaven.com/a/cotton_jersey) for jackets and sleeves.
- [Denim Fabric 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/denim_fabric_04) for trousers, Alma’s summer cap and the fine twill on the coated rain hat and collar.
- [Wool Boucle](https://polyhaven.com/a/wool_boucle) for scarves and winter caps.
